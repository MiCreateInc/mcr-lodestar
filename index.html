Open-source AI for the fight against human trafficking

Lodestar Lab is a volunteer tech lab inside MiCreate. We build AI agents that take routine work off nonprofit staff, and research machine learning that helps prevent trafficking. Every tool is free, and every model is measured before anyone relies on it.

Two kinds of work

One track automates the routine work that eats nonprofit staff time. The other studies how machine learning can help prevent trafficking without putting anyone at greater risk.

AI agent automation

Agents that read, draft and check routine work like translation, reports and request routing, then wait for a person to approve before anything is sent.

      How we work

      Every agent asks before it acts.

      Agents draft and suggest. A person approves anything that is sent, published or assigned, and every step is logged.

      Every model is measured.

      Nothing ships without a held-out evaluation, broken down by language and group, that anyone can rerun.

      Every tool is free and open.

      Code, evaluations and safe datasets are public, so other organizations can reuse them at no cost.
