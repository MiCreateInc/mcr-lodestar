About the lab

A volunteer lab that turns MiCreate's anti-trafficking mission into working software.

Why a lab

MiCreate offers free legal and business support to creative entrepreneurs who have experienced human trafficking, and teams up with local organizations working against it.

Organizations like these do critical work with almost no technical staff. Hours go to translation, formatting and reporting, and tools like resource finders or risk screening rarely get built for them.

The lab is the next step: free, open-source tools partner organizations can actually use. It runs on donated cloud credits and volunteer time, so it costs MiCreate nothing to start. All code, accounts and data belong to MiCreate, and every project ships with documentation so the work can continue if any volunteer steps back.

Principles

First six months

Targets, not deadlines. The lab runs on 4 to 6 volunteer hours a week and slows down before anyone's day job does.

    Pilots start only after the data and ethics policy is signed off. Public releases wait for partner feedback.

    Impact

    Reported to MiCreate every quarter. Tracking starts with the first release.

    Roles

    Join the lab

    Everyone contributes publicly under a pseudonym, so contributing never exposes who you are.

    • Your public profile shows no real name, photo, employer or location.
    • You commit with GitHub's private no-reply email.
    • MiCreate leadership keeps a confidential roster and can confirm your role in writing when you need it.
    • You sign a short volunteer agreement covering these principles before your first contribution.
