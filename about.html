About the lab

A small volunteer tech team that gives long-term volunteers a place in MiCreate's work.

Why a lab

MiCreate offers free legal and business support to creative entrepreneurs who have experienced human trafficking, and teams up with local organizations working against it.

Many people would gladly give their tech skills to this work; they just don't know there is a place for them. The lab gives long-term volunteers that place: clear projects, a regular rhythm and handbooks so the work carries on.

It starts from work already under way, the website and Slack chatbots and onboarding automation, and adds projects only as volunteers join. It adds no new cost beyond the tools MiCreate already uses. All code, accounts and data belong to MiCreate.

    Roles

    Join the lab

    Everyone contributes publicly under a pseudonym, so contributing never exposes who you are.

    • Your public profile shows no real name, photo, employer or location.
    • You commit with GitHub's private no-reply email.
    • MiCreate leadership keeps a confidential roster and can confirm your role in writing when you need it.
    • You sign a short volunteer agreement covering these principles before your first contribution.
